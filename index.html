Before you launch

  • Wear a PFD and a leash. Carry a VHF radio or a phone in a dry bag, plus a whistle.
  • Tell someone ashore your launch spot and when you'll be back. Paddle together.
  • You need a valid DFFE recreational angling permit (sold at the Post Office). Check current bag and size limits before you go.
  • Respect MPA no-take zones, and check the Shark Spotters status in False Bay.
  • Offshore wind is the big killer. If the wind blows off the land, stay close or stay home.
  • Forecasts are model data and can be wrong. Look at the water yourself before you launch.
